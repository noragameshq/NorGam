Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

At NoraGames.io, safeguarding your privacy and the privacy of children is our highest priority. We design our educational games, printables, and STEM toys with safety and pedagogical principles in mind.

1. Information We Collect

We do not intentionally or directly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you make a purchase via our official Etsy or Shopier stores, your payment and shipping details are securely processed by those respective platforms. We do not store or process your credit card information on our servers.

2. Use of Contact Information

Any email address or name provided via our native contact form is strictly used to respond to your explicit inquiry or support request. Your data is never shared with, rented to, or sold to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

The data you share through our contact form is transmitted securely to our team via professional third-party infrastructure (FormSubmit) solely for the purpose of operational correspondence.

3. Parental Rights & Children's Privacy

We fully comply with international children's privacy regulations (including COPPA and GDPR-K). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has inadvertently provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately. We will take swift action to verify and permanently remove such data from our records.

4. External Links

Our website contains links to external platforms, including our social media profiles (Instagram, Pinterest) and external marketplaces (Etsy, Shopier). Once you leave NoraGames.io, we do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our gaming ecosystem, please contact us at: info@norakidslab.com
