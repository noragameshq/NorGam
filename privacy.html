Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

We do not collect personal identifying information from children. If you make a purchase via our Etsy or Shopier stores, your payment and shipping details are securely processed by those platforms. We do not store your credit card information on our servers.

2. Use of Information

Any email address provided via our contact form is strictly used to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share your contact details with third parties.
